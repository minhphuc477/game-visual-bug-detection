ABSTRACT
Pulsartimingarraycollaborations,suchastheNorthAmericanNanohertzObservatoryforGravita-
tional Waves (NANOGrav), are seeking to detect nanohertz gravitational wavesemittedbysupermas-
sive black hole binaries formed in the aftermath of galaxy mergers. We have searched for continuous
waves from individual circular supermassive black hole binaries using NANOGrav’s recent 12.5-year
data set. We created new methods to accurately model the uncertainties on pulsar distances in our
analysis, and we implemented new techniques to account for a common red noise process in pulsar
timing array data sets while searching for deterministic gravitational wave signals, including contin-
uous waves. As we found no evidence for continuous waves in our data, we placed 95% upper limits
on the strain amplitude of continuous waves emitted by these sources. At our most sensitive fre-
